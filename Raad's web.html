Mahram, mahram is an unmarriageable family member with whom marriage is permanently forbidden in Islam. And non-mahram is vice-versa. So, we have to make the non-mahrams/gayere mahrams as our life partner. Muslims are commanded to lower their gaze to avoid looking at non-mahrams,

Allah the Almighty says,

'وَقُل لِّلْمُؤْمِنَاتِ يَغْضُضْنَ مِنْ أَبْصَارِ‌هِنَّ'- 'And say to the believing women that they must lower their gazes'(An-Nur:31)

It doesn't mean that only womens have to lower the gaze/sight, the order is same as on the men.

Again our Prophet (SM) has said,

'If one of you were to be stabbed in the head with an iron needle, it would be better for him than touching a woman whom he is not permitted to touch(Sahee Al-Jaami:5045)

Yes in this time it is likely impossible to us to not to look to our teacher, boss, collegues we have to interect with them, but we should know our limits, we can talk to them with a lower gaze. We often stare at the non-mahrams involuntarily, but we have to look away immediately and that is okay with that.
